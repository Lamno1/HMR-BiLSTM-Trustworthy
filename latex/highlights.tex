\noindent\textbf{When Validation Fails to Rank Recurrent Architectures: A Controlled Study of Inter-Patient ECG Classification}

\medskip
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em,itemsep=4pt]
  \item A residual-memory LSTM cell is tested against matched recurrent controls.
  \item Its gain over a matched cell vanishes once in-cell dropout is removed.
  \item Loss configuration and cell implementation explain most of the baseline gap.
  \item Under subject-grouped CV the cores tie and validation cannot rank them.
  \item Better supraventricular ranking gives no risk-controlled selective operating point.
\end{itemize}
